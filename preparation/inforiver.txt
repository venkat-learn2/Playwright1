> I worked on a product called Inforiver. It is an advanced reporting and analytics solution built on top of Microsoft Power BI.

> Normally Power BI is mainly used for dashboards and visualization, 
but Inforiver extends its capabilities by providing advanced features like financial reporting, 
planning, and data analysis without writing any code.

> In BFSI domain, this is very useful because banks and financial institutions deal with a large amount of data such as customer transactions, 
loan details, branch performance, and financial reports. 

> Using Inforiver, 
  business users can create detailed reports, analyze financial data, and generate insights directly inside Power BI.

> For example, banks can build dashboards to monitor loan portfolios, analyze revenue, track branch performance, 
and forecast future financial trends.

> So overall, Inforiver helps organizations convert raw business data into meaningful reports and dashboards, 
which helps management take better business decisions
